# About the Author

**Charan Yadav [cleo25] — Poet, observer, and collector of feelings.**

I write about the things that stay with us — people who leave a mark, dreams we dare to hold, moments we wish could last a little longer, and feelings that are sometimes difficult to explain.

For me, poetry is a way of giving those feelings a voice. Some words come from memories, some from imagination, and others from moments I cannot quite put into words any other way.

This repository is my personal poetry archive — a place to preserve my original writing, its earliest versions, and the little pieces of life that inspired them.

Every poem here holds a fragment of a thought, a feeling, or a moment worth remembering.

I'm not here to have all the answers. Sometimes, I just want to give a feeling somewhere to stay.

### Why This Archive Exists

- To preserve my original poems and their earliest available versions.
- To document the development and revision of my writing.
- To create a dated, accessible record of my work.
- To share my words with readers while retaining authorship and ownership.

### A Little Note

Some things are too fleeting to hold onto.

So I write them down.

And perhaps, long after the moment has passed, the words will still be here.

---

*Some feelings become words. Some words deserve to stay.*

— Charan
